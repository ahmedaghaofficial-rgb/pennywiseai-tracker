# Personal Money Tracker — Product Scope v1

## Goal
A mobile-first app that becomes the single reliable record of personal and simple-business money movement.

Every transaction should answer:
- How much?
- Money in or money out?
- When?
- From where?
- To whom?
- How was it paid or received?
- Why?
- Which profile: Personal or Business?
- Is there a note, reference, or proof?

## In scope
- Personal / Business profiles
- Cash, bank, card, and wallet accounts
- Income / Expense / Transfer / Investment
- SMS and bank-notification capture
- Manual entry
- AI-assisted entry
- CSV / PDF import
- Categories / sub-categories / tags
- Budgets
- Recurring transactions / subscriptions
- Search / filters / analytics
- Attachments
- Backup / restore
- Multi-currency
- Automation rules

## Out of scope for now
- ERP
- Inventory
- Payroll
- CRM
- HR
- Purchase orders
- Full accounting
- Double-entry accounting
- Complex approval workflows
- Team permission systems

## Product rule
A feature belongs only if it helps answer:
"Where did the money come from or go, when, how, to whom, and why?"
